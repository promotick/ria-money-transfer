Gift card artwork (prototype)
=============================
Each <brand>.svg is a generated card: the brand logo on a coloured card.
Logos were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons (file pages are public domain "simple logo",
flagged "trademarked"). The brand names and logos remain trademarks of their owners; confirm
brand usage with RIA / the gift card provider before any public release.

amazon      File:Amazon_logo.svg
walmart     File:Walmart_logo_(2008).svg        (2008 logo, not the current one)
target      File:Target_Corporation_logo_(vector).svg
starbucks   File:Starbucks-wordmark-green.svg   (wordmark only)
apple       File:Apple_logo_black.svg
googleplay  File:Google_Play_2022_logo.svg
netflix     File:Netflix_2015_logo.svg
uber        File:Uber_Eats_2020_logo.svg
doordash    File:DoorDash_Logo.svg
homedepot   File:The_Home_Depot_logo.svg
bestbuy     File:Best_Buy_logo_2018.svg
spotify     File:Spotify_logo_with_text.svg

Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:<name above>
To replace an image with the provider's official artwork, overwrite <brand>.svg (any 380x380 SVG)
or change img() in js/proto.js to point to a PNG/JPG.
